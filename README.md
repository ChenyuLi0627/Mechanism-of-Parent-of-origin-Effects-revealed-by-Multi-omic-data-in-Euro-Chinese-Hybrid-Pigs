# Mechanism-of-Parent-of-origin-Effects-revealed-by-Multi-omic-data-in-Euro-Chinese-Hybrid-Pigs
Code of analysis during study of "Mechanism of Parent-of-origin Effects revealed by Multi-omic data in Euro-Chinese Hybrid Pigs"
# 1.Introduction
Multi-omics phasing analysis with trio families and long read lengths using different inbred lines. The aim was to explore the unbalanced gene expression differences between different parental origins, imprinted gene mining and the potential epigenetic regulatory mechanisms underlying them.
# 2.Main content
## Multi-omic phase analysis of hybrid pigs and identification of phase-specific signals    
* Short-read and Long-read whole genome sequencing alignment of parents and offspring, SNP calling, trio-based phased SNP identification that used for phasing anaylsis of multi-omic.
* RNA-seq aligenment, quantification of phased RNA-seq data, PSE identification.
* GM-seq  aligenment, CG site detection of phased GM-seq data, PSMR identification.
* Cut&tag aligenment, peak calling, classification of peaks by different assay, consensus peaks construction, phased peaks identification and filtration with non-phased consensus peaks, PSHM and PSCTCF identification.
* iso-seq aligenment, transcript identification, transcriptions panel construction, phased transcriptions quantification.
* Hi-C seq aligenment, the highest resolution calculation, phased contact map contruction, identification of phaed higher-order chromatin structures including compartments, TADs and loops, PSTAD boundary identification.
